The importance of being earnest is not merely a matter of manners,
but of sincerity and the subtle art of conversation.

Algernon remarked that the truth is rarely pure and never simple.
Jack replied that one must be serious about something in life,
if one wishes to have any amusement at all.

Cecily kept a careful diary, in which every trivial event was recorded
with surprising precision.

In the margin of the manuscript appeared a whimsical token: bunburiosity.
