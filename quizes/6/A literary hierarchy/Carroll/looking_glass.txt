One thing was certain, that the white kitten had had nothing to do with it.
It was the black kitten's fault entirely. For the white kitten had been
having its tea very quietly, and had not spilt a drop.

Alice felt that something was not quite right in the room. The mirror seemed
to ripple, and the fire gave off a peculiar glow, as if it were breathing.

She stepped closer, and suddenly everything went strange and sideways,
almost as if the world had become a slithy place full of odd reflections.

A scribble appeared in the margin: snarkish and jabberlog.
