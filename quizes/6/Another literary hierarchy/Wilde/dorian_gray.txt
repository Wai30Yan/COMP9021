The studio was filled with the rich odor of roses, and when the light summer wind
stirred amidst the trees of the garden, there came through the open door the heavy
scent of the lilac, or the more delicate perfume of the pink-flowering thorn.

Lord Henry lay back in his chair, smoking a cigarette, and watching the
graceful movements of the artist as he worked upon the portrait.

The picture had a strange vitality. It seemed to watch him as much as he watched it.

In one corner, a faint and almost invisible word had been etched: aesthetica.
