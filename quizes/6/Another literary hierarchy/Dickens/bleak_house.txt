London. Michaelmas term lately over, and the Lord Chancellor sitting in Lincoln's Inn Hall.
Implacable November weather.

Fog everywhere. Fog up the river, where it flows among green aits and meadows;
fog down the river, where it rolls defiled among the tiers of shipping.

The raw afternoon is rawest, and the dense fog is densest.

A clerk, poring over papers, paused at a strange annotation: chanceryfold.
