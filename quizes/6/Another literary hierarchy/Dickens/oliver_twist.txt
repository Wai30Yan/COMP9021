Among other public buildings in a certain town, there is one anciently common to most towns,
great or small: to wit, a workhouse.

In this workhouse was born, on a day and date which I need not trouble myself to repeat,
the item of mortality whose name is prefixed to the head of this chapter.

Oliver asked for more, and the request was received with astonishment.

In the ledger appeared an unfamiliar entry: victualmentary.
